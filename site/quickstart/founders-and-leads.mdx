Three things the kit does for you: tells you what is happening in the codebase in plain words, measures how far the team can trust agents, and turns the team's review feedback into checks that enforce themselves.

Nothing to install: everything below works from [claude.ai/code](https://claude.ai/code), [chatgpt.com/codex](https://chatgpt.com/codex), or either phone app. [How to start](/how-to/use-the-kit-from-the-browser).

## Your first three tasks

<Steps>
  <Step title="See what shipped">
    ```text
    /weekly-review for every author on main, not just me
    ```
    A week of commits grouped into bug fixes, tech debt, and new work. Without "every author" it covers only your own commits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure how much you can trust agents">
    ```text
    /trust-audit
    ```
    A score from 0 to 3 on each layer of the trust ladder, with evidence, the five fixes that would help most, and how many agents can safely work in parallel today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn review feedback into rules">
    ```text
    /style-guide-from-reviews last 90 days
    ```
    Finds the feedback reviewers keep repeating and writes it into STYLE.md with an example of each. Engineers then move each entry to a stronger layer with `/correct`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
